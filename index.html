Ways to Play: The Rules

Pickleball is easy to learn! Here are the core rules you need to know.

The Serve

Must be hit underhand and below the waist, served diagonally across the court.

Double Bounce

The ball must bounce once on each side before any volleys are allowed.

The Kitchen

No volleying (hitting the ball out of the air) while standing inside the non-volley zone.

Scoring

Only the serving team can score points. Games are typically played to 11, win by 2.
