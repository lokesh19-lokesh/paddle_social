Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about playing at Paddle Social.

Do I need to be a member to play?
No! While members receive benefits like discounted rates and early booking access, guests are always welcome to book courts or drop into open play.
Do I need to bring my own paddle and balls?
You are welcome to bring your own equipment. However, we offer premium paddle rentals at the front desk, and high-quality outdoor/indoor balls are available for purchase or rent.
What is your cancellation policy?
Court reservations can be cancelled for a full refund up to 24 hours in advance. Cancellations made within 24 hours will not be refunded.
Can I bring my own coach?
Outside coaching is strictly prohibited. We have a roster of vetted, highly trained professionals available for lessons and clinics.
